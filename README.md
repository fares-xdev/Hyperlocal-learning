# HDS — 2-Day Software Engineering Crash Course

Welcome to the **HDS Software Engineering Crash Course**, specially tailored for Front-End Developers evolving into **Software Engineers & AI Architects** capable of building, evaluating, and leading the **Hyperlocal Delivery System (HDS)** platform.

---

## 🎯 Course Objective

The goal is **NOT** to turn you into a traditional backend specialist who memorizes programming syntax.

The goal is to make you a **Software Engineer who can effectively direct, review, and collaborate with AI Agents**:

* Understand system architecture and data flow.
* Model relational databases and business domain entities.
* Design state machines for complex order lifecycles.
* Reason about geospatial dispatch, driver matching, and real-time tracking.
* Audit financial flows, merchant settlements, and commission logic.
* Critically review AI-generated code, detect dangerous edge cases, and challenge architectural flaws.
* Intelligent evaluation of existing platforms (**Fleetbase** vs. **ERPNext**).

---

## 🧭 Course Structure

The course spans **17 Sessions** split across **2 Days**:

* **DAY 1 — Backend & System Foundations** (Sessions 01 – 06)
* **DAY 2 — HDS Core Systems & Architectural Evaluation** (Sessions 07 – 17)

## 🚀 How to Start a Session

1. Check your current status in `PROGRESS.md`.
2. Open the corresponding session file in `sessions/SESSION-XX.md`.
3. Prompt your AI Learning Agent:
   > *"I am ready to start SESSION-XX. Please act according to `LEARNING-METHOD.md` and guide me through the concepts and interactive exercises for this session."*
4. Complete the interactive exercises, answer Socratic questions, and satisfy the **Exit Target** to advance to the next session!
